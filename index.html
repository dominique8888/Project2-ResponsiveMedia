Featured Landmark

The Wonder Wheel is the iconic ferris wheel at Deno’s Wonder Wheel Amusement Park in Coney Island, Brooklyn. Standing 150 feet tall, it has been a centerpiece of the boardwalk skyline since its debut in 1920.

Coney Island has been a hub of seaside amusement for more than a century, known for its thrilling rides, bright lights, and summertime energy. From the historic Cyclone roller coaster to modern attractions, it remains one of New York Citys' most beloved destinations for fun by the beach.
